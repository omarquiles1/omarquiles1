I am a 4th-year Industrial Technologies Engineering student at the University of Oviedo, working on renewable energy, heavy industry and fluid simulation. I spent my 3rd year on Erasmus at Gazi University (Ankara, Turkey). I bridge the gap between traditional mechanical/industrial engineering and applied software development, utilizing data and coding to optimize physical systems.

### 🔭 Current Focus
*   **Research Scholar at IUTA (FERES Group):** Aerodynamic and structural analysis of vertical-axis wind turbines using MATLAB.
*   **Hardware & Automation:** Designing and implementing industrial electronics, such as ESP32-based buck converters and PLC systems.
*   **Infrastructure & Fluid Dynamics:** Programming hydraulic simulation models for plumbing and fire protection systems.
